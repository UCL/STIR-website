Organisers

  • Daniel Deidda, National Physical Laboratory, UK
  • Nikos Efthimiou, University of Groningen, Nederland
  • Charalampos Tsoumpas, University of Groningen, Nederland
  • Ludovica Brusaferri, London South Bank University, UK
  • Kris Thielemans, University College London, UK

Where and when

This year the event will take place on Thursday, 12 November, (time TBC), Granada, Spain as a Special Event during the IEEE NSS-MIC. Remote attendance will be possible. More information to follow.

Overview

During this annual meeting users and developers presented their recent work with STIR with the emphasis on software and algorithmic development. In this meeting, we encourage participation and sharing. Early and preliminary works are welcomed.

We look forward to meeting you in Granada!
Daniel, Nikos, Harry, Ludovica and Kris

(For up-to-date information on this meeting please always check the STIR website.)

Registration and abstract submission

Deadline for abstract submission is passed.

Registration is required for in-person attendance. Please check the official IEEE NSS/MIC registration to the event with a very small charge.

If you are interested in presenting your recent work using and/or extending the STIR library, submit an abstract via the official conference website (Submit a new contribution). Although the abstract submission is closed for the main conference, it is still open for the workshops including our meeting. If your abstract is accepted, it will feature as part of the IEEE conference in the official program of the meeting. (Note that as the conference allows virtual attendance, you can also present remotely in this workshop).
